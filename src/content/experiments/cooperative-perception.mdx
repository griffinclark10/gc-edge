## From plan A to CoAlign

The goal was to add intermediate fusion (spatial- and channel-adaptive feature fusion) to the open-source DAIR-V2X codebase, then compare it against the early and late fusion that codebase already shipped. DAIR-V2X is the only public dataset that is both real-world and vehicle-to-everything: about 70,000 LiDAR point clouds and camera frames from cars and roadside units.

Plan A didn't survive contact with the code. Every error I fixed in the DAIR-V2X pipeline (mmdet3d, pypcd, a conversion to KITTI format) surfaced another, and contacting the authors and filing issues on their GitHub didn't unblock it. In March 2023 the CoAlign authors released a cooperative-perception model with intermediate fusion for DAIR-V2X built in, so I switched.

CoAlign encodes each agent's point cloud with PointPillars, corrects the relative pose between the vehicle and the roadside unit with an agent-object pose graph, then fuses their feature maps before detection. I trained its early, late and intermediate fusion variants (the intermediate model for 30 epochs) and scored all three at AP@0.5 and AP@0.7.

The work ran in PyTorch with CUDA on the Queen's computing cluster, supervised by Dr. Farhana Zulkernine at BAMLAB.
