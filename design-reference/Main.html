Cooperative perception for autonomous vehicles

EXP. 002 · CAPSTONE

Queue Hop: is the line worth it?

A point-based P2PNet crowd counter aimed at the line outside the campus engineering bar, piped to a React Native app with live counts and wait estimates. Science in service of thirst.

EXP. 003 · QMIND · BEST PAPER, CUCAI

HelpingHand: ASL, letter by letter

Real-time American Sign Language letter recognition from a webcam feed, built with Keras and OpenCV. The accompanying paper took Best Paper at the Canadian Undergraduate Conference on AI.
