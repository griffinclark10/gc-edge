§1 · HYPOTHESIS

Foundation models mislabel things. A model a thousand times smaller has no capacity to ignore those mistakes — so it learns them.

§2 · QUESTIONS

  1. How much accuracy does a microcontroller detector lose when trained on teacher labels instead of human ones?
  2. Does that gap shrink or grow as the student gets smaller?
  3. How many frames does the feedback loop need to send back to close it?
